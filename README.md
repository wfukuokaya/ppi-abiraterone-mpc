# Proton pump inhibitors and abiraterone acetate in metastatic prostate cancer

R code for the analysis reported in:

> Association between concomitant proton pump inhibitor use and survival of patients with metastatic prostate cancer receiving abiraterone acetate: a post-hoc analysis of pooled data from three randomized controlled trials. *Prostate Cancer and Prostatic Diseases*. <https://www.nature.com/articles/s41391-023-00695-x>

Pooled individual participant data from the LATITUDE, COU-AA-301, and COU-AA-302 trials; restricted mean survival times compared between proton pump inhibitor users and non-users with inverse probability of treatment weighting.

## Contents

`script.R`: analysis.

## Requirements

R 4.x with `finalfit`, `ggplot2`, `ggsci`, `survey`, `tableone`.

## Data

Patient-level data are not included in this repository.
